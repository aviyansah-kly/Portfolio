GOOD TO KNOW

A few things worth knowing.

Is intlife suitable for landed developments?

Yes. We work with property developers to plan smart living experiences around landed-home specifications and homeowners' needs.

Can features be tailored to our project?

Supported devices, security options, connected scenes and handover requirements can be discussed based on the development scope.

How does energy fit into Smart Living?

Energy awareness and solar-ready planning can complement the smart home. Any specific solar, battery or app integrations require project-level technical confirmation.

When should we involve intlife?

Early planning gives us more room to align devices, infrastructure and homeowner experience with your development goals.
