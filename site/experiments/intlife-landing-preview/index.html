COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions developers often ask.

Quick answers to common questions about smart living for landed residential projects.

Can we start with just a show unit?

Yes. intlife supports show unit and sales gallery experiences. The setup can be discussed around your project's requirements and rollout plans.

Which smart home features are available?

Options include smart lighting, air-conditioning, smart locks, scene automation, zone control and app control. Final features depend on your project scope and device compatibility.

Can solar PV and BESS be part of our project?

Solar PV and battery storage can be discussed alongside smart-home planning. Equipment selection, engineering feasibility, energy monitoring and any connection to the intlife app must be confirmed for your project.

When should we involve intlife?

Ideally during the planning stage, so device requirements and installation can be coordinated early. Ongoing developments can also be discussed.

What happens at homeowner handover?

intlife offers homeowner handover support for developer projects. We can discuss the app setup and handover process based on your deployment scope.
