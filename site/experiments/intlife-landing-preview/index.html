07 / COMMON QUESTIONSBEFORE WE GET STARTED

Questions before
your next project?

Here are a few things developers may want to understand before discussing smart living.

Ask the intlife team
Can we start with a show unit before a full development?

intlife supports developer show units and sales galleries. The appropriate scope can be discussed based on your development plans.

Which smart living features can be included?

Options include compatible smart lighting, air-conditioning, door locks, security and app-based control. Final capabilities depend on the selected devices and project integration.

When should we discuss smart living integration?

Early planning helps your team consider cabling, device compatibility, installation and homeowner handover. Existing development requirements can also be reviewed.
